🪔 ABOUT WSDC

More Than A Dance Academy

A place to move, connect, learn and grow.

WSDC is a dance, fitness and wellness academy based in Buxar, Bihar, created for people who want to become more active, confident and energetic.

We believe that dance is not just about learning steps. It is about expressing yourself, building confidence, meeting people and enjoying every moment of movement.

Whether you are a beginner taking your first dance class, a fitness enthusiast looking for an energetic workout, or someone simply wanting to try something new, WSDC welcomes you.

Our classes are designed to create a positive, friendly and motivating environment where everyone can learn at their own pace.

From dance and Zumba to yoga and choreography, our goal is to bring movement and wellness into everyday life.
